# Making more Paige pictures

Paige's current picture is `assets/host/idle.webp` (originally `art/source/paige-fullbody.webp`). To give her different moods, make extra pictures in the **same style** and save them next to it:

| File | Mood |
| --- | --- |
| `idle.webp` | Default, chatting (already there) |
| `thinking.png` | Working out a tricky request |
| `printing.png` | Job sent to the printer |
| `error.png` | Something went wrong |

- **Full body, standing, on a transparent background**, the same framing as the current picture (about 1122 × 1402), so she doesn't jump around when her mood changes.
- `.png`, `.webp`, `.jpg` or `.gif` all work. Keep each file under about 2 MB.
- After adding them, click back into the app. Type `/mood thinking` (etc.) in the chat to check each one.

## Keep her consistent

Use the current picture (`art/source/paige-fullbody.webp`) as the reference/character image for each new one (most tools have "use as reference", "character reference" or "vary" options), keep the same seed if your tool shows one, and only change the expression and pose line.

Don't name real games, studios or artists in the prompt: describing the style keeps Paige original and avoids copying anyone's character.

## Base description (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Glossy semi-realistic pin-up illustration, same character as the reference image: Paige, a glamorous woman with long wavy blonde hair, blue eyes, winged eyeliner, glossy lips, silver hoop earrings and a pink heart necklace. She wears a shiny black and hot-pink cropped racing jacket with crown logos, a black mini skirt with a pink heart charm, a thigh strap and black platform lace-up boots with pink laces. Flirty, cheeky, fun, never explicit. Full body, standing, transparent background.

## Mood lines (add one after the base description)

**thinking.png**
> Pose: one index finger tapping her chin, head tilted slightly. Expression: eyes glancing up and to the side, lips pursed, mischievous and thoughtful.

**printing.png**
> Pose: holding up a freshly printed sheet of paper in one hand, a couple of pages flying around her. Expression: big delighted laugh, eyes squeezed happily, celebrating.

**error.png**
> Pose: one hand pressed to her forehead in mock dramatic despair. Expression: wide eyes, worried eyebrows, a little "oops" pout, still glamorous.

## Tips

- If a tool makes her too revealing or too plain, nudge the words "tasteful", "fully clothed" or "low-cut" up or down; she should be flirty, never explicit.
- If hands come out wrong, regenerate just that image or crop a little tighter.
- Keep a copy of your originals somewhere else; this folder is part of the app.
